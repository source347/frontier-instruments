04 / MODEL NOTES

Operational concepts

Encoding
Converting an observation into a durable memory representation, with metadata such as source, timestamp, and importance.
Retrieval
Selecting candidate memories in response to a query, then ranking them under relevance and resource constraints.
Decay
Reducing effective accessibility over time. Decay may be offset by reinforcement or importance.
Consolidation
Combining overlapping records into more compact representations while retaining useful provenance.
Context selection
Choosing a bounded subset of memories to expose to the model for a particular inference step.

All records are local synthetic examples. Refreshing the page resets the simulation; no data is transmitted or persisted.
